# imports/ — IMMUTABLE

Source material, kept at the **workspace root** — deliberately outside `.joserah/`.

The owner drops PDFs, statements, screenshots, articles and exports here; `/joserah:import`
copies sources in verbatim under a dated folder, `imports/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<short-label>/`. The AI
may read all of it, but never edits, rewrites or summarizes anything here in place. Synthesis
goes to `.joserah/knowledge/wiki/`.

**This folder is never backed up.** It holds bank statements, credit reports, vendor PDFs and
firmware — originals the owner already has elsewhere, and material that must not travel into a
git history. Keeping it outside `.joserah/` is what makes the backup scope safe by construction:
the backup is `.joserah/` and nothing else.

`work/` is the one sub-folder that is not immutable: scratch a tool or an agent leaves behind is
parked there so it stays out of the backup; it may be deleted.
